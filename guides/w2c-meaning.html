W2C meaning: links, finds, and where to cop (before SuperBuy)

W2C usually means “want to cop”—someone wants a buy link for an item, often from Taobao, Weidian, or 1688. A w2c link is not a purchase by itself; you still paste the listing URL into a shopping agent. On superbuyget we route browse to Street Style; checkout stays on superbuy.com.

W2C vs w2c spreadsheet vs rep spreadsheet

Discord posts, Reddit threads, and w2c spreadsheets are shortcuts to the same thing: rows of marketplace URLs plus photos. A rep spreadsheet or replica spreadsheet is the same habit with different branding—dense link grids people skim before they copy a permalink.

  • W2C finds = individual links people share when asked “where to cop?”
  • W2C website = any browse UI that feels like a sheet (categories, search, thumbnails)—not the agent checkout.
  • Rep finds on superbuyget = our curated shortlist plus filters on the home grid; the full store search lives on Street Style.

Where to cop reps without treating chat as gospel

“Where to cop reps” answers should end in a live listing you reopen the day you pay, not a screenshot from last season.

  1. Pick a lane (shoes, hoodies, bags) on Street Style or use our on-site shortlist.
  2. Open the SKU page and copy the product permalink—not a Discord image.
  3. Paste into SuperBuy search (see numbered paste steps).
  4. After warehouse intake, read QC photos for reps before you approve shipping.

Looking for a specific silhouette? Use the store search (e.g. “dunk”, “hoodie”) instead of hunting trademark-heavy “w2c {brand}” posts that age out overnight.

How to buy reps with SuperBuy (one-line map)

Agents exist because Chinese marketplaces assume domestic wallets. SuperBuy pays the seller, stores the parcel, shoots warehouse QC, and quotes export lines—you handle paste, balance, and shipping choices on their site. superbuyget.com never charges cards.
